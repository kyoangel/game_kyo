Chrono Rush時光疾走勇者

Race against the ticking clock! Power-level through monster zones, buy gear in villages, and storm the Demon Castle before time runs out. Spend Crystals at Camp for permanent Roguelike upgrades and conquer higher World Loops.與倒數時鐘賽跑！在怪物荒野中極速練等、進村秒買裝備、衝進魔王城討伐魔王！每局結算魔晶永久強化營地天賦，挑戰更高輪迴的世界！

How to play玩法

Move with WASD / Arrow Keys (or drag on touch). Press Space to Dash. Bump into monsters for instant high-speed combat, visit villages to pause the clock, heal, and upgrade gear, and slay the Demon Lord before time expires!使用 WASD 或方向鍵移動（手機可拖曳搖桿），空白鍵衝刺。撞擊怪物自動高速戰鬥，進村莊暫停計時、回滿血並強化裝備，在時間歸零前衝進魔王城擊敗魔王！
