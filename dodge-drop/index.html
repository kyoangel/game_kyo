Neon Rush霓虹極速

Neon blocks rain down. Dodge them, graze near misses for combos, grab stars for coins and unlock new skins.霓虹方塊從天而降，閃避它們、擦邊連擊、收集星星換金幣，解鎖全新造型。

How to play玩法

Drag anywhere to move (arrow keys work too). Slip close to a block for a near-miss combo, grab stars for coins to unlock skins, and pick up a shield to survive one hit.在畫面任意處拖曳移動（也可用方向鍵）。貼近方塊閃過可累積擦邊連擊，收集星星換金幣解鎖造型，拾取護盾可抵擋一次撞擊。
